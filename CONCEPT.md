# Button Game — Creative concept

## Creator's direction

Button Game is a tactile, fast-paced game centered on tapping targets, reacting quickly, collecting and combining numbers, and pursuing high scores. Its character comes from the relationship between timing, visible numerical feedback, sound, and the escalating scoring system.

The project is intended to stand on its own and later appear in the interactive babuni.dev portfolio, with the game and its development story accessible to visitors.

## Established design elements

- Spawned targets with rotation, hit feedback, and delayed shattering.
- Floating score numbers that can collide, merge, and be collected.
- Quick Strike and active 2× scoring.
- Special 21→42 and 37→73 combinations.
- Time bonuses and golden time transfers.
- Compact Points, Time, Reaction, and APM displays.
- A faded live leaderboard during gameplay and a full leaderboard at Game Over.
- Android-hosted local Wi-Fi sessions with shared scores.

These are descriptions from the existing development record, not a new specification.

## Exploratory concepts, not approved

Red/trap buttons, Bluetooth hosting, true peer-to-peer multiplayer, and an in-game score reset have been discussed as possible future directions. None should be implemented without an explicit creator decision.

## Portfolio integration

Preserve the playable game independently. The portfolio should link or embed a suitable build and present the concept, development milestones, screenshots, technical architecture, and human–AI collaboration history. Embedding approach, performance budget, and visitor interaction remain undecided.
