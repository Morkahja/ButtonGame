# Human–AI collaboration and attribution

Button Game is directed by its human creator. AI tools may assist with code, testing, troubleshooting, technical writing, and reconstruction of development records. Assistance does not transfer authorship of the underlying creative vision.

Each substantial entry should capture:
- Creator's request, reasoning, and constraints.
- AI interpretation, alternatives, and technical contribution.
- Creator approval, rejection, correction, or postponement.
- Actual files/commits changed.
- Automated and physical verification, including unresolved work.

Retrospective summaries must be labeled as such. Do not invent conversations, approvals, tests, or attribution. Do not publish private chat transcripts, credentials, or personal information. The intended portfolio presentation will show how the work evolved, not merely the final result.
